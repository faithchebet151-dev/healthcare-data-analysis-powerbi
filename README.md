# Healthcare Data Analysis Project

## Project Overview

This project explores a healthcare dataset containing **55,500 patient records**. It uses descriptive analysis and an interactive Power BI dashboard to examine recorded medical conditions, patient demographics, admission types, and billing amounts.

The aim is to demonstrate practical healthcare data analysis skills and communicate patterns in a dataset clearly.

## Research Questions

1. Which medical conditions appear most frequently in the dataset?
2. How are patient records distributed by gender?
3. How are records distributed across admission types?
4. What is the average age represented in the dataset?
5. What is the total recorded billing amount?

## Tools and Skills

- **Microsoft Excel:** data exploration and summary statistics
- **SQL:** querying and analyzing records
- **Microsoft Power BI:** interactive dashboard and visualizations
- **Analytical skills:** descriptive statistics, data interpretation, and communicating findings

## Key Findings

- **Records analyzed:** 55,500
- **Average age:** approximately 51.54 years
- **Most frequently recorded condition:** Arthritis, with 9,308 records (approximately 16.77% of all records)
- **Gender distribution:** 27,774 male records and 27,726 female records
- **Most common admission type:** Elective, with 18,655 records
- **Total recorded billing amount:** approximately 1,417,432,043 dataset currency units

The billing currency and the meaning of the billing field have not been independently verified.

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard includes:
- KPI cards for total records, average age, and total billing amount
- A chart showing records by medical condition
- A chart showing records by gender
- A chart showing records by admission type

## Limitations and Responsible Interpretation

- The findings describe this dataset only. They should not be treated as estimates of disease prevalence in Kenya or any other population.
- The dataset's source, collection method, and representativeness have not been established.
- The analysis is descriptive and does not establish causes or effects.
- The currency and interpretation of billing amounts require verification.
- Any real-world healthcare decision would require validated data, context, and appropriate privacy safeguards.
